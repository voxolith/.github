# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Voxolith to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their
age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most friction is a misunderstanding.
- Critique code and ideas, never the person who wrote them.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it specifically and constructively.
- Respect people's time: search before asking, and keep reports and replies focused.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or flooding issues and pull requests with low-effort or
  machine-generated content.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every Voxolith space: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and
reviews, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@AleBles](https://github.com/AleBles)) may remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may block anyone from
the organisation, temporarily or permanently.

To report a problem, open an issue asking the maintainer for a private contact (without the
details), or use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or user concerned. Reports are
handled privately and as promptly as a one-person project allows.
